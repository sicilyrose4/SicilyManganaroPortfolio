Workshop Social Block - Internship Project II

A drag-and-drop block for adding social media links to emails in Workshop’s modern email editor.

ROLE
Product Design Intern, Designer
TEAM
Me, another designer, and engineers
TIMELINE
About 3 weeks, summer 2026

Problem

The new email editor had no way to add social links, and customers were asking for one.

Users needed a social block as simple as the rest of the editor.

Solution

Drag in the social block, then manage every network from one drawer: pick it, paste the link, reorder, resize.

How the design evolved

In Workshop’s editor, every block is set up in a side drawer. Here’s how the social block’s drawer changed across four versions.

V1

Icon grid on its own screen

HOW IT WORKED

Pick an icon from a grid, then a new screen opens to add its link.

THE PROBLEM

Adding three links meant three trips back and forth.

V2

Icons and fields in one card

WHAT CHANGED

The icon grid, URL, and title all live together, one card per link.

WHY

Fewer clicks, and you can see the whole set at once.

V3

A dropdown instead of a grid

WHAT CHANGED

The icon grid became a dropdown.

WHY

The grid took up too much space in the drawer.

Final

One row per network

WHAT CHANGED

Each link is a row that collapses, under Styles and Visibility tabs.

WHY

A cleaner list, drag-to-reorder, and tabs that match the editor’s other blocks.

A closer look at my thought process - making links draggable

Reordering links was the detail I spent the most time on. The drawer is narrow, so the drag handle had to be easy to find without crowding each link’s controls.

Handles that were always visible made three links look heavy in a small space, and the team agreed it felt busy. Moving the handle outside the card and showing it on hover keeps each card clean: expand, icon, name, and delete, with nothing competing.

The final handle appears when you hover a link, darkens under the cursor, and turns blue while you drag.
